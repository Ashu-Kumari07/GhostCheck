How GhostCheck works

GhostCheck compares what a posting says with what the employer's own website shows, and explains every point of the score.

Five steps

  1. Read the posting

    We extract the title, company, location, posted date, pay and description.

  2. Open the careers page

    A web agent visits the employer's own site and searches for the role.

  3. Look for duplicates and reposts

    We check whether the same role appears repeatedly or in many places.

  4. Check hiring news

    We look for recent layoff or hiring-freeze reports about the company.

  5. Combine the signals

    Each signal is weighted and merged into one score with a confidence level.

Score bands

0-34 Likely Real 35-64 Uncertain 65-100 Likely Ghost

Limits

The score is an estimate, not a fact. Some sites block automated checks, and a role may be filled privately without ever being posted. When a signal cannot be checked, we skip it and lower the confidence label.
